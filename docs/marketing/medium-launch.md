*Talk to the Claude you already run — and watch it answer on screen.*

---

If you use **Claude Code**, you already know the feeling: you're deep in a flow, and you keep
switching to a terminal, typing a question, and scrolling back through a wall of text to find
the one line you needed. It works. But it's not how Tony Stark talks to JARVIS.

So I built **Orrin** — a small macOS app that gives the Claude you *already run* a **voice**
and a **screen**.

**→ [dockndevai.github.io/orrin-site](https://dockndevai.github.io/orrin-site/)**

Orrin isn't another AI. It's the wiring — ears, a mouth, and a canvas — around the Claude
Code already installed on your Mac. You talk to it. It answers out loud. And instead of a
wall of text, the answer *renders* — a clock when you ask the time, a weather scene when you
ask the forecast, a diagram when you ask how something works.

## The idea: your Claude, hands-free

Most voice assistants *are* the brain. Orrin is the opposite. It brings the voice and the
screen and uses **your** Claude as the brain — so everything you've already set up in Claude
Code just works, spoken:

- Your **MCP connectors** (Gmail, Drive, GitHub, your files)
- Your **subagents** and **skills**
- Your project's **`CLAUDE.md`**

Nothing to reconfigure. Orrin loads your Claude config and drives it.

## How it works

1. **Say your wake word** (or click the mic). Your microphone is transcribed *on your Mac*
   with a local speech model — nothing leaves the device to start.
2. **It runs your Claude.** Orrin drives the Claude Code on your machine, in the project
   you're working in, with all your connectors and context.
3. **It speaks and shows.** You hear the answer and watch it render on a JARVIS-style canvas.
   The full transcript waits in a drawer until you want it.

That last part is the fun one. Ask *"what's the capital of Japan?"* and you get a clean
card — **Tokyo**, region, population — not a paragraph. Ask *"how does a REST API work?"* and
you get a labeled **diagram**. The screen does the reading for you.

## Private by design

This matters, so I'll be blunt about it: **there is no Orrin cloud.** Your microphone audio
is transcribed **on your device**. Only the resulting text prompt goes to *your* Claude —
exactly as if you'd typed it. Orrin doesn't run a server that sees your conversations, your
audio, or your files.
